# Roadmap

**Now: polish the whole path end to end.** Upload → preview the author approves → sales page → daily lessons → payment. Everything below builds on that path working without hand-holding.

Then three bets for scale. The web version is [site/roadmap.html](site/roadmap.html).

| Bet | What it means | First step |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Any inbox, not just email** | The same drip, delivered wherever the reader already reads: WhatsApp, Slack, Telegram, SMS. If it can receive text, it can receive a lesson. The reader picks the channel at sign-up. | Add one channel after email. Pick the one readers ask for most. WhatsApp only lets businesses start a conversation with pre-approved templates, so it takes more setup than Slack or Telegram. |
| **2. Listen as well as read** | Each lesson as a 5 to 10 minute audio clip, for commutes and walks. A private podcast feed per reader would work in any podcast app. | Get audio rights in the author agreement: they're separate from ebook rights. Then voice one book as a pilot. |
| **3. Grow the catalog** | **(a) List books before authors join.** A book page with the title, the author, a link to the author's own free sample and a link to buy from the author. Readers can ask to be told when it becomes a drip. **(b) Invite authors to upload themselves.** Self-serve upload (see [docs/upload-flow.md](docs/upload-flow.md)), plus outreach from the prospect list. | List the 50 prospects' books, then show each author how many readers asked for their book. |

**Why the catalog matters most:** more books bring more readers, and readers asking for a book are the best reason for its author to join. Keep contacting authors directly. Listings help the outreach, they don't replace it.

**Guardrail for 3a:** a listing only shows facts about the book and links out to the author's own pages. Don't host, convert or excerpt a book's text until the author has agreed. If an author asks for their listing to come down, take it down.
