Privacy

Last updated 14 October 2026.

What we collect

When you ask for a preview, we receive the email address and book link you type into the form. That is all. This site has no analytics, no tracking pixels and no cookies.

What we do with it

  • We use your email to reply to you about Dripbook and your preview.
  • We use your book link to read your public sample and build the preview.
  • We do not sell your details or add you to anyone else's list.

Who else handles it

Form submissions pass through our form provider, which stores them so we can read them. Fonts on this site load from Google Fonts, so your browser shares your IP address with Google when the page opens.

Keeping and deleting it

We keep your details until you ask us to remove them. Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} to see, correct or delete what we hold about you.
