Privacy

Last updated 14 October 2026.

What we collect

When you upload your book, we receive the PDF and the name and email address the upload page asks for. That is all. This site has no analytics, no tracking pixels and no cookies.

What we do with it

  • We use your email to tell you when your sales page is ready, and to talk to you about your book.
  • We use your PDF only to build your course. It stays yours, and we never share or publish it without your go-ahead.
  • We do not sell your details or add you to anyone else's list.

Who else handles it

Uploads go through a Dropbox file request, so Dropbox stores your PDF and the details you enter until we move them. Fonts on this site load from Google Fonts, so your browser shares your IP address with Google when the page opens.

Keeping and deleting it

We keep your details until you ask us to remove them. Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} to see, correct or delete what we hold about you.
