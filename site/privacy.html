Privacy

Last updated 21 November 2026.

Dripbook sends books to your inbox, a part at a time. This page says what we keep, why, who else handles it, and how to have it deleted.

What we keep

When you start a drip: your email address, the book, your pace, the time zone your browser reports, when you signed up and confirmed, and how many parts we've sent. If you don't confirm within 7 days, we delete the sign-up.

When you ask for a book: the title, the author, your email address, whether you wrote it, and when you asked. We use it to see which books readers want, to ask their authors or publishers, and to tell you when one arrives. To drop accidental double requests, we keep a one-way fingerprint of your IP address with the request for 10 minutes. We never store the address itself.

When you drip your own PDF: the PDF opens in your browser and is not uploaded. When you press Email it to me, we receive the text of your lessons, the pictures in them (cut from the pages in your browser), the title and your email address. We use them only to send your lessons; the pictures travel inside each email, so they stay in your inbox and nothing loads from us when you open it. Nobody reads them unless you ask us for help, nothing is shared or published, and they are never used to train AI. We delete them 30 days after your last lesson, or after 7 to 8 days if you never confirm.

When you send us your book as an author: your name, email address, PDF and saved cuts. We use them to build your course and to talk to you about it, and we publish nothing without your go-ahead.

What you confirm

  • A PDF you drip to yourself is one you may use this way: you wrote it, you own the rights, or you have permission.
  • A book you send us as its author is yours to publish: you wrote it or hold the rights, and it doesn't infringe anyone else's.

If you think something sent through Dripbook infringes your rights, email us and we will remove it.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, advertising trackers or cookies on this site.
  • No open or click tracking in our emails.
  • We don't sell your details, share them, or add you to anyone else's list.

The PDF editor keeps your draft cuts in your browser's storage, on your device. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Who else handles it

  • Vercel hosts the site and runs sign-ups. Its logs see your IP address. Its private file storage (Vercel Blob) holds the pictures from your own PDF until we delete them.
  • Upstash holds our database: sign-ups, requests and your own lessons.
  • Postmark sends our emails and keeps a record of each one for up to 45 days.
  • Dropbox receives author uploads until we move them.
  • Cloudflare serves the How it works video and sees your IP address if you play it.
  • jsDelivr serves the editor's PDF reader, and Google Fonts serves our fonts. Both see your IP address when a page loads.

These companies may handle your data outside your country.

Stopping and deleting

Every drip email has an unsubscribe link, which stops it at once. To see, correct or delete everything we hold about you, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.
