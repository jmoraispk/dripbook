Questions

Do you shorten or rewrite the books?

Never. You get every word of the original, in order. We only decide where each day's part ends, always at a natural break like the end of a scene or chapter.

How long is each part?

You choose: about five, ten or twenty minutes of reading. A ten-minute part is around 2,300 words. You can change your pace at any time.

Can I read faster on some days?

Yes. Every email has a link to get the next part straight away, so you can binge a chapter on a slow Sunday.

Can I read more than one book at a time?

Yes. Each book you start arrives as its own email, so a novel and a philosophy book can run side by side.

Which books are in the library?

A thousand of the most-read classics, from Pride and Prejudice and Moby Dick to Walden, The Republic and The Wealth of Nations. Books by living authors are coming next.

What if I stop enjoying a book?

Stop it from the link at the bottom of any email, and start another whenever you like.
