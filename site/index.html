How it works

From the book you wrote to a course you sell.

  1. 1Upload your PDFDrop in the ebook you already have. Dripbook reads its chapters and headings.
  2. 2Review your lessonsWe split it into short emails that each end at a natural stopping point. Rename subject lines, merge or split lessons, and set the pace.
  3. 3Set your pricePick what a reader pays. We suggest $49 to start. Choose how many lessons are free as a preview.
  4. 4Share one linkPost it to your audience. Dripbook handles checkout and sends every lesson on schedule.

Questions creators ask

What kinds of books work best?

Non-fiction that teaches: programming, productivity, business and self-help. Anything a reader works through rather than skims.

Do I need my own email list?

No. You share one link. Readers sign up on your course page, and Dripbook sends every lesson.

Who owns the reader list?

You do. Export your readers any time and take them wherever you like.

Can I sell a book I already sell as a PDF?

Yes, as long as you hold the rights. The email course can sit right next to your existing PDF.

What happens when a reader finishes?

The last lesson can point them to whatever you offer next: another book, a coaching call, or your community. You set it up in your dashboard.

What does it cost?

Your first book is free to set up. After that, Dripbook keeps a 10% share of each sale. There is no monthly fee.
