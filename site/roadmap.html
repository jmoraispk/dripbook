Roadmap

Where Dripbook is going.

A book, a little at a time, wherever you already are. Here is what we are building first, and the three bets that come after it.

Now

First, every step works end to end.

Before anything new, the whole path from a PDF to a paying reader has to run smoothly, without anyone holding it together by hand.

  1. UploadThe author sends a PDF.
  2. PreviewThe author sees the lessons and approves them.
  3. Sales pageOne link to share with readers.
  4. Daily lessonsSent on schedule, paused or read ahead at will.
  5. PaymentReaders unlock the rest; the author gets paid.

Next

Three bets for growing.

Bet 1

Any inbox, not just email

If it can receive a message, it can receive a lesson. Readers pick where the drip arrives when they sign up.

EmailWhatsAppSlackTelegramSMS

First stepAdd one channel after email: the one readers ask for most.

Bet 2

Listen as well as read

Every lesson as a 5 to 10 minute audio clip, for the commute or a walk. It arrives in the reader's own podcast app.

First stepVoice one book as a pilot, with the author's go-ahead for audio.

Bet 3

A catalog of self-published books

Browse books by independent authors, read the author's own free sample, and buy straight from them, even before a book becomes a drip. Readers can ask for it as a drip, and authors can claim their book and upload it themselves.

First stepList the first 50 books and show each author how many readers want theirs.

Why the catalog matters most.

More books→More readers→Readers ask for books→More authors join↺

Readers asking for a book are the best reason for its author to join. A listing only shows facts about the book and links to the author's own pages, and any author can have theirs taken down.
